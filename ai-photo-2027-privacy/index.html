Privacy Policy

AI Photo 2027 is an on-device photo editor for Android, funded by advertising. This policy separates the two things that happen on your phone: your pictures never leave the device, and Google’s ad SDK does exchange data. Both are described below, in plain terms.

1Summary

  • Your photos are edited entirely on your device. They are not uploaded, not stored on any server, and not used to train anything.
  • We have no account system, no profile, no analytics and no crash reporting. We hold no copy of anything about you.
  • The app is free because it shows advertising served by Google AdMob. That SDK can process an advertising identifier, approximate location and ad-interaction data under Google’s own privacy terms — see section 3, which is the part of this policy that concerns other people’s data processing rather than ours.
  • Outside the EEA, UK and a few US states, Google’s consent form is not shown; inside them it is, and your choice there controls what the ad SDK may use.

2What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The app never sends your photos, your gallery contents, your name, your contacts or a usage report to us. There is no server of ours for an editor to talk to.

The two network calls the app makes are both described below: the ad requests in section 3, and the version check in section 4.

3Advertising (Google AdMob)

AI Photo 2027 integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK. It shows three ad surfaces, and only three:

  • one labelled native ad inside the photo library list,
  • one banner at the foot of the photo library,
  • one full-screen “app open” ad when you return to the app after leaving it for a while — never on the first launch, and never inside the editor or the camera.

For those ads, Google and its advertisers may process:

Your choices

  • Consent form: if you are in the EEA, the UK or a US state with its own consent law, Google’s own consent screen appears on the first launch. It is Google’s User Messaging Platform, and its settings are honoured for every later ad request.
  • Personalised ads: Google Settings → Privacy → Ads (adssettings.google.com).
  • Limit ad tracking / reset the ID: your device’s Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Opt out of interest-based advertising across apps: aboutads.info/choices and networkadvertising.org/mobile-choice.

Choosing any of these does not remove the ad slots from the app — unpersonalised or region-appropriate ads take their place.

4Version check

On start, the app reads one small public file (desho2050.github.io/Gisco/…) that carries the newest released version number. If the store has a newer build than the one installed, the app asks you to update before it opens.

The request carries no photo data and no identifier of ours; it is an ordinary HTTPS GET, so the hosting provider sees the standard technical fields (IP address, user agent, timestamp) in its own access logs. We do not receive or store those logs.

5Your photos

  • Photos are loaded from your gallery through the Android system photo picker, or handed to the app by the system “Open with” / “Share” sheet.
  • Every edit — background blur, skin retouching, virtual makeup, face reshaping, hand smoothing, semantic recolouring, cartoon style, magic eraser and auto-crop — is computed on the device by the models in section 6.
  • Edited pictures are written back to your own gallery folder Pictures/AI Photo 2027/ through Android’s MediaStore. They are never uploaded.
  • The camera viewfinder keeps frames in memory only until you accept one. Nothing is recorded to a hidden file and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • An ad is never drawn on top of a photo you are editing, and no photo pixel is ever handed to the ad SDK.

6On-device machine-learning models

The app ships seven open, pre-trained models from Google’s public model catalogues:

  • MediaPipe Face Landmarker and Hand Landmarker (.task)
  • MediaPipe Selfie Segmenter and DeepLab V3 semantic segmentation (.tflite)
  • BlazeFace short-range face detection (.tflite)
  • Object-removal inpainting and a cartoon-style network (.tflite)

They run entirely inside the app process on your device’s CPU or GPU. No image is passed to a service, no inference request leaves the device, and no model is retrained on your data.

8Data deletion and retention

We keep nothing, so there is nothing for us to delete on request; the honest answer is a local one:

  1. Delete the edits — remove the files in Pictures/AI Photo 2027/ from your gallery or the Photos app.
  2. Clear app data — Android Settings → Apps → AI Photo 2027 → Storage → Clear data removes the app’s own settings and its saved consent choice.
  3. Uninstall the app — Android deletes the app’s private storage with it.

For the advertising data in section 3, deletion and retention are Google’s to answer; the choices and contact routes are in Google’s Privacy Policy. If you write to us about anything in sections 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 or 8, we respond within 30 days.

9Children

AI Photo 2027 is a general-audience photo editor. It is not directed at children, it is not registered as a family app, and the ad requests it makes are not tagged as child-directed. We collect no data from anyone knowingly, and the app has no messaging, social or user-generated content feature that could expose a child to other users.
